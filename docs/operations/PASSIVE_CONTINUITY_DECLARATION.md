# Passive continuity declaration

**Transition:** Active custodianship → **passive continuity stewardship**.

Philosophy: *The final responsibility of mature engineering is knowing when continued intervention would reduce, rather than improve, institutional stability.*

Prerequisites: [TERMINAL_STEWARDSHIP_NOTE.md](TERMINAL_STEWARDSHIP_NOTE.md) · [INFRASTRUCTURE_FINALIZATION_RECORD.md](../architecture/INFRASTRUCTURE_FINALIZATION_RECORD.md)

---

## Infrastructure state

The newsroom is:

- **calm** — quiet digest, boring runtime when healthy  
- **bounded** — persistence and telemetry within policy  
- **transferable** — snapshot, checklist, capsule current  
- **institutionally legible** — frozen surface documented  
- **operationally sufficient** — `OPERATIONALLY_COMPLETE`  

Validation continues. Architecture does not expand.

---

## Stewardship shift

| Phase | Primary responsibility |
|-------|------------------------|
| Active custodianship (closed) | Observe, record, intervene rarely |
| **Passive continuity (current)** | **Non-intervention** unless evidence proves risk |

Infrastructure **runs**. Engineering activity **mostly stops**. Survivability and validation **remain**.

---

## Expected future

**Months without architectural changes** = normal operations.

Weekly validation and monthly review may continue as **light observation** — not as a development program.

Thresholds: [maintenance_thresholds.md](maintenance_thresholds.md) · guidance: [long_horizon_custodian_notes.md](long_horizon_custodian_notes.md).

---

## Final position

Stewardship officially moves from **active architecture care** to **passive continuity preservation**.
