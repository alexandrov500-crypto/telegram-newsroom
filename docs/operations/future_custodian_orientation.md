# Future custodian orientation

One page for custodians arriving **years** after dormancy. Not a platform. Not a ritual catalog.

Seal: [CONTINUITY_SEAL.md](../architecture/CONTINUITY_SEAL.md) · dormancy: [INSTITUTIONAL_DORMANCY_DECLARATION.md](INSTITUTIONAL_DORMANCY_DECLARATION.md)

---

## If the infrastructure still works

**Prefer preserving it.**

Working calm infrastructure is the goal state, not a prelude to redesign.

---

## If validation remains calm

**Avoid intervention.**

Stable `weekly_runtime_validation` and quiet digest are not requests for improvement.

---

## If intervention is required

Keep it:

- **bounded** — smallest proven fix  
- **reversible** — clear rollback  
- **minimally invasive** — no new layers  
- **continuity-preserving** — architecture unchanged in spirit  

Classes: [maintenance_thresholds.md](maintenance_thresholds.md). Bar: [engineering_restraint_charter.md](engineering_restraint_charter.md).

---

## If nothing requires action

**No action is the correct stewardship action.**

Do not intervene because:

- the repo feels “too quiet”  
- other projects are active  
- governance could be “cleaner” or “more modern”

## Final custodian reminder (stillness)

If the system **remains calm**, **quiet operation itself** is the strongest evidence that stewardship is succeeding.

Do not disturb calm to prove you are still stewarding.

Protocol: [STEWARDSHIP_SILENCE_PROTOCOL.md](STEWARDSHIP_SILENCE_PROTOCOL.md).

## Philosophy

*The final achievement of mature infrastructure is the ability to remain quietly trustworthy even in long periods of deliberate inactivity.*

*The clearest sign of mature infrastructure is often the absence of any need to visibly change it.*
