# Operational stillness

**Meaning:** Calm infrastructure should not seek activity.

Protocol: [STEWARDSHIP_SILENCE_PROTOCOL.md](STEWARDSHIP_SILENCE_PROTOCOL.md)

---

## Calm infrastructure

Stable infrastructure **should not seek activity**.

Visible motion is not a health metric. Boring runtime is.

---

## Quiet continuity

**Operational continuity** matters more than **visible evolution**.

Trust is earned by dependable quiet operation, not by changelog frequency.

---

## Engineering patience

**Absence of architectural motion** may indicate **institutional health**.

Do not confuse stillness with stagnation. Stillness with calm validation is **success**.

---

## When stillness breaks

Intervene only when evidence shows:

- survivability risk  
- boundedness violation  
- security requirement  
- compatibility failure  

Not when silence feels uncomfortable.
