# Engineering restraint charter

**Status:** Official operational doctrine for steady-state newsroom.  
**Scope:** Preservation phase — not engineering evolution.

Philosophy: *The final form of mature infrastructure is not continuous evolution, but quiet endurance.*

Related: [continuity_covenant.md](continuity_covenant.md) · [ARCHITECTURAL_COMPLETION.md](../architecture/ARCHITECTURAL_COMPLETION.md) · [infrastructure_custodianship.md](infrastructure_custodianship.md) · [operational_time_capsule.md](../architecture/operational_time_capsule.md) · [STEADY_STATE_STATUS.md](STEADY_STATE_STATUS.md)

---

## Principles

| Principle | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| Calmness over activity | Quiet weeks are success |
| Preservation over optimization | Do not tune mature calmness |
| Evidence over curiosity | Measurement before motion |
| Bounded maintenance over expansion | Surgical fixes only |
| Silence over operational noise | Invisible digest, boring logs |

**Intervention is the exception. Non-intervention is the default.**

---

## Anti-patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why it violates restraint |
|--------------|---------------------------|
| Improvement without evidence | Curiosity is not justification |
| Architecture restlessness | Freeze exists for a reason |
| Governance revival | Chain is complete |
| Observability expansion | Cohesion layer is sufficient |
| Optimization by boredom | Activity ≠ value |
| Cleanup for emotional comfort | Requires boundedness proof |
| Intervention for reassurance | Psychology is not ops evidence |
| Artificial engineering momentum | Hibernation is healthy |

---

## Intervention threshold doctrine

> **Every intervention must justify why non-intervention is unsafe.**

Change is allowed **only if all** apply:

1. **Measurable operational evidence** exists  
2. **Survivability risk** if unchanged  
3. **Bounded repair path** (surgical, reversible)  
4. The newsroom **cannot safely remain unchanged**

Otherwise: **preservation wins.**

Combine with: [stewardship_dormancy.md](stewardship_dormancy.md) · [operational_preservation_mode.md](operational_preservation_mode.md).

## Calm infrastructure recognition

**Healthy infrastructure:**

- is boring  
- is quiet  
- changes rarely  
- survives long periods untouched  
- does not demand attention  
- does not generate engineering anxiety  

If the newsroom stays calm for **months** — that is **not stagnation**. It is **maturity**.

## Long-term preservation horizon (6–12 months)

**Do not plan:**

- roadmap expansion  
- governance redesign  
- maturity evolution  
- observability growth  

**Permitted only:**

- evidence-based maintenance  
- survivability repair  
- security fixes  
- continuity preservation  
- brief documentation freshness  

---

## Hard constraints

**Forbidden:** stewardship resurrection, recursive maturity, operational AI layers, architecture modernization, telemetry ecosystem growth, optimization without degradation, intervention for psychological reassurance.

**Allowed:** bounded repair, emergency intervention, continuity protection.

---

## Expected outcome (6–12 months)

Newsroom remains:

- calm, readable, transferable, bounded  
- operationally boring  
- free of architectural change pressure  
- dignified through **restraint**, not churn

## Passive continuity reinforcement

Stewardship has transitioned to **passive continuity** — see [PASSIVE_CONTINUITY_DECLARATION.md](PASSIVE_CONTINUITY_DECLARATION.md).

**The absence of engineering activity is evidence of architectural success**, provided:

- survivability remains intact  
- runtime validation stays calm  
- persistence stays bounded  
- digest stays quiet  

Do not replace calm systems for novelty. Do not reopen roadmap planning.

---

## Final operational goal

Do not “improve” the newsroom further.

Prove that mature infrastructure preserves **stability**, **value**, and **legibility** through **disciplined non-intervention**.
