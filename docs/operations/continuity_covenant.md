# Continuity covenant

Binding **operational intent** for custodians — not a legal contract, not automation.

Philosophy: *A completed infrastructure is not one that cannot evolve, but one that no longer needs to.*

Declaration: [ARCHITECTURAL_COMPLETION.md](../architecture/ARCHITECTURAL_COMPLETION.md) · finalization: [INFRASTRUCTURE_FINALIZATION_RECORD.md](../architecture/INFRASTRUCTURE_FINALIZATION_RECORD.md) · sunset: [stewardship_sunset_boundary.md](stewardship_sunset_boundary.md) · restraint: [engineering_restraint_charter.md](engineering_restraint_charter.md)

---

## Principles

| Principle | Commitment |
|-----------|------------|
| Preservation over novelty | Calm weeks are success |
| Restraint over activity | No momentum for its own sake |
| Continuity over momentum | No roadmap expansion |
| Bounded maintenance over expansion | Surgical fixes only |
| Calmness over optimization | Do not tune mature quiet |

---

## Main covenant

> **The newsroom may remain unchanged indefinitely unless evidence proves intervention necessary.**

Intervention requires justification of why **non-intervention is unsafe** — see [engineering_restraint_charter.md](engineering_restraint_charter.md).

---

## Custodian commitments

- Observe weekly; review monthly — existing scripts only  
- Update [STEADY_STATE_STATUS.md](STEADY_STATE_STATUS.md) honestly  
- Preserve transferability — [stewardship_transfer_checklist.md](stewardship_transfer_checklist.md)  
- Do not revive governance expansion without defect proof  
- Treat engineering inactivity + calm runtime as **healthy**

---

## Quiet endurance horizon (12+ months)

**Success signals:**

- no architectural pressure  
- no governance expansion  
- no stewardship recursion  
- no operational anxiety  
- calm validation · quiet digest  
- minimal intervention  
- preserved transferability  

**Officially healthy:** engineering inactivity while quality holds.

---

## Forbidden without evidence

- governance resurrection  
- maturity layering  
- operational AI expansion  
- orchestration systems  
- observability ecosystems  
- optimization for reassurance  
- modernization without degradation proof  
- intervention from engineering restlessness  

---

## Permitted

- bounded repair  
- emergency intervention  
- continuity preservation  
- security maintenance  
- brief documentation freshness
