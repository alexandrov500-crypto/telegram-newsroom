# Stewardship silence protocol

**Not a new phase.** Operational stillness acceptance atop dormancy readiness.

Philosophy: *The clearest sign of mature infrastructure is often the absence of any need to visibly change it.*

Related: [operational_stillness.md](operational_stillness.md) · [INSTITUTIONAL_DORMANCY_DECLARATION.md](INSTITUTIONAL_DORMANCY_DECLARATION.md)

---

## Silence is not neglect

If validation remains calm, bounded, and survivable — **silence is evidence of maturity**, not abandonment.

Low commit activity is **compatible with** `STEWARDSHIP_SILENCE` ([README.md](../../README.md)).

---

## Intervention burden

Every intervention must prove **why continued silence is unsafe**.

Default question: *What breaks if we do nothing?*

If the answer is unclear — **preserve silence**. See [maintenance_thresholds.md](maintenance_thresholds.md) · [engineering_restraint_charter.md](engineering_restraint_charter.md).

---

## Healthy operational pattern

**Weeks or months without architectural changes** are expected operational behavior.

Forbidden responses to silence:

- activity for reassurance  
- governance revival “because it’s quiet”  
- modernization for visibility  
- “keeping the project alive” initiatives  

---

## Final position

The newsroom has entered **stewardship silence** — continuity matters more than visible engineering motion.
