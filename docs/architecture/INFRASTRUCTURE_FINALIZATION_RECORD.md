## Why expansion stopped

Additional architectural complexity **no longer produces proportional operational value.**

Further maturity layers risk:

- governance recursion  
- observability sediment  
- engineering restlessness disguised as improvement  

The newsroom **officially exits active evolution.**

---

## Accepted tradeoff

**Prioritize:**

- stability  
- legibility  
- boundedness  
- calmness  
- transferability  

**Deprioritize:**

- novelty  
- abstraction growth  
- optimization activity without degradation evidence  

This is intentional, not a backlog failure.
